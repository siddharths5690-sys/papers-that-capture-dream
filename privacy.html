Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you place an order and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

When you order, we may collect your name, WhatsApp/phone number, delivery address, product and quantity, customization or delivery notes, UPI UTR/reference number, and order details.

2. Why we use it

We use this information to process and deliver your order, verify payment, communicate with you about the order, provide customer support, and maintain basic order records.

3. WhatsApp

After checkout, the website prepares an order message for WhatsApp. You must choose to send that message. Your phone number and order details are not displayed publicly to other customers.

4. Payment information

We do not ask for or store your UPI PIN, bank password or OTP. Payment verification is performed using the payment information available to the store and the UTR/reference number you provide.

5. Sharing

We do not publicly display customer phone numbers or addresses. Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary to fulfill an order, provide delivery, comply with legal obligations, or protect the business and customers.

6. Data retention & contact

We may retain order information for bookkeeping, customer support and business records. For privacy questions or requests, contact Prosperweb29@gmail.com.
